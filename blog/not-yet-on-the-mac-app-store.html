Not Yet Is Out Now on the Mac App Store

2 min read

Not Yet: Checkout Blocker is out now on the Mac App Store. It's a free download for any Mac running macOS 14 or later, and it works in Safari. iPhone and iPad are still on the way, and they'll come from the same App Store listing.

What You Get on the Mac

The same Not Yet that's been on Chrome, now in Safari. When you reach a checkout or payment page, Not Yet pauses it until you retype a freshly generated code, character for character. Not a countdown, and not a snooze button. Everything that protects you is free: checkout blocking, the code unlock, your Quiet Place, time limits on sites, and the basic recap. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing sent to a server.

Turning It On in Safari

Safari keeps new extensions off until you say so. After you install Not Yet:

  1. Open the Not Yet app once.
  2. In Safari, open Safari → Safari Extensions… and check the box next to Not Yet.
  3. Set its website access to Always Allow on Every Website, so it can see checkout pages wherever you shop.

If it isn't pausing anything, Setup & Help walks through the usual fixes.

Premium on the Mac

Premium is optional, and it's bought in the Not Yet app with your Apple Account: monthly, or lifetime for $39.99 once. It adds Guard Mode, your own reflection prompts and colors, the Quiet Place pinboard, the full weekly recap, and widgets. Already bought lifetime? Copy code for Chrome in the app unlocks Premium in Chrome too, at no extra cost.

iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad version is still on its way through the App Store. It's the same listing, so when it's ready the same link will work there too. Until then, the Mac app and the Chrome extension are both ready now.
