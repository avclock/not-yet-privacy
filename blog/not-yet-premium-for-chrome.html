Not Yet Premium for Chrome: One Key, Yours for Good

3 min read

Not Yet's checkout blocking is free on Chrome, and it always will be. If you want more, Premium is one purchase: $39.99, once, for a key that unlocks Premium in Chrome on any computer you use. No subscription, no account, nothing to renew.

What Premium Adds

  • Guard Mode. A PIN you choose before anything protective can be loosened: turning off a category, removing a site, or making the pause easier.
  • Your own reflection prompts, in your own words, instead of the built-in questions.
  • Your own colors. A gradient theme from two colors you pick, and a background photo.
  • The Quiet Place pinboard, for photos of what you're saving toward.
  • The full buy-button shield, which also covers one-click wallet and Buy Now, Pay Later buttons like Apple Pay and Klarna.
  • The full recap: a 14-day trend, a breakdown by category, and your mood log.

Everything else stays free: every category, automatic checkout detection, the code, reflection, and wait unlocks, time limits on sites, and the Quiet Place itself.

How to Get Your Key

  1. Choose Get a Premium key, here or on Not Yet's Premium page in Chrome.
  2. Pay on Payhip's checkout page. Payhip handles the payment, not Not Yet.
  3. Your key arrives by email. It starts with NY2. Keep that email: the same key works in Chrome on every computer you use.

How to Turn Premium On

  1. In Chrome, click Not Yet in the toolbar and choose Open Settings.
  2. Under Unlock Not Yet Premium, open Already have a Premium key or App Store code?
  3. Paste your key and choose Activate Premium. Premium turns on right away.

Premium needs Not Yet 1.1 or later. Chrome keeps extensions up to date on its own; to check, open chrome://extensions and look at Not Yet's version number.

Already Paying for Not Yet on Apple Devices?

A lifetime Premium purchase in the Not Yet app for iPhone, iPad, or Mac covers Chrome too, so you never pay twice. In the app, tap Copy code for Chrome (or Email it to myself), then paste the code into the same box in Chrome. It's Apple's own signed record of your purchase, and Chrome checks it the same way it checks a key.

Still No Server

Your key is checked right on your computer with a digital signature, so activating it sends nothing anywhere. There's still no account and no Not Yet server, and nothing about you is collected. And because Premium is one payment, there's nothing to lapse: Guard Mode stays on until you turn it off with your own PIN.

Lost your key, or one won't activate? Write to us with the email you bought with. Refunds go through Payhip.
