Frequently asked questions

Is Shotstand free?

Yes. Making a full set and saving it at any size is free, with no account, no watermark, and no limit. Shotstand Premium is an optional one-time purchase of $0.99 that adds saving every size at once, more languages, templates, photo backgrounds, the wide image, and Apple Watch.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It's one app on all three, so Premium bought on one is yours on the others with the same Apple Account (tap Restore purchase if it doesn't show right away).

Which screenshot sizes does it make?

iPhone 6.9" (1320 × 2868) and 6.5", iPad 13" (2064 × 2752), Mac (2880 × 1800 and 1440 × 900), and Apple Watch (422 × 514, 416 × 496, and 396 × 484). Those are the sizes App Store Connect scales down for every smaller device.

Do my screenshots get uploaded anywhere?

No. Shotstand has no account and makes no network requests. Your screenshots and projects stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I use it for an app that isn't mine?

Yes, as long as you have the right to use the screenshots you put in it. Agencies and freelancers can make sets for clients the same way.

Is it affiliated with Apple?

No. Shotstand is an independent app. The device frames are drawn by the app itself, not copied from Apple's artwork.

Something isn't working. Where do I get help?

The Support page covers the common questions, and there's a Submit feedback button in the app's Settings, under Help. Or email avclock@protonmail.com.
