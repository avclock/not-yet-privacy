Why Not Yet exists

Impulse doesn't wait for a timer to run out

A countdown just asks you to wait it out, which most people do without thinking about anything in particular. A "snooze" button is worse: tap it once and the moment's already gone. Not Yet works differently. It shows a random code and asks you to retype it, character for character, before a checkout or payment page will continue. Nothing is cached or reused: reload the page and you get a brand new code. The point is a genuine pause, not a puzzle you solve once and remember.

Frequently asked questions

Is Not Yet free?

Yes. The core blocking, the code unlock, and your Quiet Place are all completely free on both Safari and Chrome, with no account required. A Safari-only Premium unlock adds Guard Mode, custom themes, a full recap, widgets, and more.

Does Not Yet collect my data?

No. Not Yet has no server and makes no network requests to us at all. Every setting, counter, and PIN hash stays on your own device. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Can I still buy something if I really mean to?

Yes, always. Not Yet is a friction tool, not a hard block: retyping the code, answering a reflection prompt, or waiting out a timer all let a purchase through, by design. It's meant to buy a moment of thought, not stop you outright.

What is Guard Mode?

A PIN you set yourself that protects your own settings from your own impulses, like turning off a category mid-browse. If a subscription ever lapses, Guard Mode stays fully on. Your own PIN always opens it, and a built-in master reset is the only other way in if you forget it.

Is this a treatment program?

No. Not Yet is a pause and friction tool, not a substitute for professional financial, medical, or mental health advice. If you're dealing with compulsive spending or gambling and need more than a pause, please talk to a qualified professional or a relevant support organization.

Does it work the same in Chrome?

The core blocking is identical. Chrome ships as a single, fully free build with no Premium tier at all, since Chrome has no built-in way to sell an extension without standing up a license server, and Not Yet doesn't run one.
